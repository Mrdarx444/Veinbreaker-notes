## Concept:
- 2D platformer Metroidvania fighting game. When The [[Player]] Have fast movement to Overcoming obstacles and strong combos to fight enemies and bosses using combos with (bear hands and different weapons with different properties), The game is highly focused on the [[Combat System]] that makes the player feels in control like he is the final boss but with challenge level balance between the skills and difficulty, and what makes it special is the cool [[Combat System]] that allow to the player to manipulate and create new fighting styles mixed between quick short and long ranged attacks with very small Time of anticipation (Like Dante from DMC5 (combo mixed between sword and pistol)) and gradually upgrading it with [[Ability Tree System]] , then comes the [[Movement System]] that makes the [[Player]] Move freely and smoothly and react quickly.
## Game Goal:
